# 02 — Product Vision

## Product Name

**ClausePilot**

## Tagline

**Understand the contract. Verify the evidence. Know what to ask next.**

## Vision

ClausePilot is a **Legal Decision & Evidence Workspace** — not a chatbot.

It helps users understand legal documents by:
1. Extracting and analyzing clauses with AI
2. Grounding every answer in verifiable document evidence
3. Clearly identifying missing information instead of fabricating it
4. Generating targeted questions for HR or a legal professional
5. Enabling side-by-side contract comparison

## Core Principle

Every piece of information presented to the user must be:
- **Sourced**: traceable to a specific clause, page, and quote
- **Grounded**: clearly marked as GROUNDED, INFERRED, NOT_FOUND, or CONFLICT
- **Verifiable**: the user can click through to the source text

## What ClausePilot Is NOT

- Not a law firm
- Not a source of legal advice
- Not a replacement for professional legal review
- Not a generic PDF chatbot

## Success Metrics

A user who completes a ClausePilot session should be able to say:

> "I now understand what this contract says, what it doesn't say, what I should ask about, and whether there are any parts I need a lawyer to review."

## Persistent Disclaimer

> ClausePilot provides document-based information and decision support, not legal advice. For important legal decisions, consider consulting a qualified legal professional.
